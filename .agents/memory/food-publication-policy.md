name: Food publication policy
description: The owner's requirement for substantive, consolidated food articles rather than species clones.

Publish one canonical article per food, covering puppies, adult dogs and cats together. Do not reintroduce separate species clones or use shared emergency prose to inflate substantive word counts.

**Why:** The owner explicitly required SEO repair before the redesign because the previous species-multiplied articles were too repetitive and thin.

**How to apply:** New articles need genuinely food-specific mechanisms, preparation, signs/timing, alternatives and FAQs. Preserve old species links as permanent redirects, not indexable documents. Keep dog-specific evidence distinct from uncertainty in cats.
