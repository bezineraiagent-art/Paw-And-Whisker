---
name: Inbox consent boundaries
description: Owner restrictions on submission review, outreach, and record retention.
---
The admin inbox is read-only. Do not expose or repurpose free-PDF signups as marketing consent, send emails, start paid billing, or invent review status. Preserve existing records. Any future deletion or outreach requires separate owner approval.

**Why:** The owner explicitly limited this feature to private review of Plus waitlist signups and wrong-answer reports.

**How to apply:** Keep submission sources distinct when expanding admin features. A request to review submissions is not permission to contact submitters or delete their records.
